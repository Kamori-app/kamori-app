# Security policy

Kamori is pre-MVP software and has not received an independent security audit.
Do not rely on current builds as a secure production backup.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability. Before public beta,
the project will activate `security@kamori.app` and publish its encryption key.
Until that channel is operational, security reports are not accepted through a
public fallback that could expose sensitive details.

Reports should include affected versions, impact, reproduction steps, and any
suggested remediation. Do not include real user data or secrets.

## Process

The intended targets after the private channel launches are:

- acknowledge a report within three business days;
- provide an initial severity assessment within seven business days;
- coordinate disclosure after a fix is available;
- credit the reporter when requested and safe.

Kamori does not currently operate a paid bug bounty. A reviewed safe-harbor
policy will be published before public beta.
