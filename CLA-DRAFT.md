# Contributor agreement requirements

This is not an agreement and must not be presented for signature. Legal counsel
must draft and approve the actual contributor agreement before substantial
external contributions are accepted.

The reviewed agreement must:

- leave contributors ownership and broad rights to their work;
- grant Kamori the rights needed to distribute, relicense, and offer commercial
  licenses for contributed code;
- include appropriate patent grants and representations of authority;
- cover corporate contributions and employee/contractor authorization;
- explain the component-specific AGPL/Apache license split;
- provide a recorded acceptance and versioning process;
- define treatment of generated code, documentation, and design assets.
