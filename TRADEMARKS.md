# Kamori trademark policy draft

This policy is a pre-release draft and requires legal review.

The Kamori name, logos, icons, product artwork, and related brand identifiers
are not granted under the repository's software or documentation licenses.

You may make accurate nominative statements such as “compatible with Kamori”
or “forked from Kamori” as long as they do not imply endorsement. Modified or
unofficial distributions must use a different product name and visual identity
and must clearly identify their source and modifications.

Do not use Kamori branding in a domain, application identifier, store listing,
company name, or product name without written permission.

Contact information and the final enforcement/jurisdiction language will be
added after a legal operator exists.
